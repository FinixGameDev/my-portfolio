I'm **Diogo Rocha**, also known as **Finix**: a Game Developer and Game Designer from Portugal. I recently graduated from [IPCA](https://est.ipca.pt/en/curso/digital-games-development/), where I studied Digital Games Development & Engineering.

I started making games when I was 14, toying with Unity 4. Along the way I've developed good critical thinking about game design, plus skills in 3D modelling, programming and narrative writing.

On this site you'll find games and game development tools I've made on my own, as well as projects I had the pleasure of building with a team.

## Tools I work with

Unity · C# · Godot · GDScript · Procedural Generation · Level Design · Narrative Writing · 3D Modelling
