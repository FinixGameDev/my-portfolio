My old portfolio did one job: show finished projects. But most of game development isn't the finished part. It's the messy middle, the broken prototypes, and the small things I learn along the way.

So this site is now a blog. Expect three kinds of posts:

- **Projects**: full showcases of what a game or tool is and what I used to build it.
- **Devlogs**: notes from active development.
- **Blog**: tutorials and rambles.

The site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) theme, and every post is just a Markdown file.
